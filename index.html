Qnote

A note-taking app for handwriting, typed notes, pictures and equations, on Windows and Android.

What it does

  • Write and draw on an endless page with a pen or a finger, with a highlighter and clean shapes.
  • Type in rich text boxes you can place anywhere on the page.
  • Add pictures by pasting, inserting or dragging them in, and write over them.
  • Typeset equations from LaTeX.
  • Organise notebooks, pages and subpages, and search across all of them.
  • Keep private notebooks, encrypted with a password only you know.
  • Sync between your devices through a Qnote folder in your own Google Drive.

Why it asks for access to Google Drive

Syncing is optional. If you connect Google Drive, Qnote keeps a copy of your notebooks in a Qnote folder there, so your other devices can get them.

Qnote asks for the narrowest Drive permission Google offers. It can see and change only the files it created itself, and nothing else in your Drive.

Qnote has no server of its own. Your notes go only between your devices and your Google Drive. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Availability

Qnote is a personal project, made and maintained by one developer. It isn't offered for general download.
